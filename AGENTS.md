# Project creative direction

- Use `frontend-design` as the sole creative/design skill for this site.
- Do not use Field Notes, film, video-story, Remotion, canvas-film, or technical-analysis-animation patterns unless Anish explicitly reverses this instruction.
- The Work archive is one ruled, typographic Bodies of Work index: four
  editorial buckets connect enduring work to its public forms without cards or
  a second duplicate index.
- Keep the complete Work archive two-dimensional and server rendered. Its
  temporal overview is a semantic four-line-by-year color calendar with exact
  counts and links into the same body folds. Do not use a separate plot,
  Three.js, or WebGL for archive navigation.
- Preserve an editorial, inspectable HTML experience when JavaScript or motion
  is unavailable.
- Treat `content/public/anish-content.public.v1.json` as the only deployable content input. Raw CSV/Markdown files and the exact owner bundle must remain outside deployed assets.
