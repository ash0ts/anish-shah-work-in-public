# Voice and Positioning

## Core voice

Write in Anish's first person. Address technically sophisticated readers without assuming they already know the archive.

Use this sequence when it helps:

1. **Outcome:** state the useful result or idea.
2. **Mechanism:** explain what was built, tested, taught, or organized.
3. **Proof:** attach a source, bounded measure, artifact, or concrete example.
4. **Meaning:** say why it matters without claiming more than the evidence supports.

Not every field needs all four parts. A headline may carry the outcome while nearby metadata carries proof.

## Desired qualities

- First-person, active, direct, and technically precise.
- Conversion-forward without sounding like SaaS advertising.
- Confident about documented work; explicit about limits.
- Concrete nouns and verbs: build, evaluate, trace, instrument, teach, publish, integrate, convene.
- Domain language where it adds precision: agents, evaluation, observability, traces, MCP, open source, technical education.
- One claim per sentence.
- Short enough that layout, labels, and evidence can do part of the explanation.

## Avoid

- Rhetorical questions, exclamation marks, emoji, competitor framing, and unsupported superlatives.
- Generic identity language such as “thought leader,” “technical storyteller,” or “emerging AI.”
- Inflated causality such as “drove revenue” when the record supports only pipeline association or support.
- Abstract openings such as “A public…,” “This body of work…,” or “In today's rapidly changing landscape…”.
- Repeating “public,” “work,” “body,” “form,” or “technical” when a concrete noun is available.
- Restating the same title, summary, qualifier, or methodology in adjacent modules.
- Raw importer notes, registration URLs, durations, replay status, or role-only fragments as summaries.

## Vocabulary contract

- Four archive categories: **lines of work**.
- A grouped record: **work**, **project**, **course**, or **series**, chosen by what it is.
- Nested records: **talks**, **recordings**, **translations**, **code**, or **artifacts**.
- Evidence records: **sources**.
- “Public forms” appears only where the methodology genuinely needs that term.

Doorway object names in Play are fixed: TV, Bookshelf, Music player, Record crate, Computer, and Treadmill. Put authored themes in screen headings or flavor copy, never in place of the literal doorway name. Category mappings and routes are structural.

## Editing test

Before keeping a sentence, ask:

- Does it say something the reader cannot already see?
- Is the subject and action concrete?
- Is a factual clause supported by sanitized content?
- Is its limit adjacent rather than buried elsewhere?
- Can one nearby sentence or label be deleted now?
