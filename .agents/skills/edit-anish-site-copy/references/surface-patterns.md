# Surface Patterns

Treat the typed copy-field registry as the source of truth for exact field names and maximum lengths. These patterns govern intent and density.

## Home

- Keep initially visible Home copy at or below roughly 140 words.
- Use one thesis, one supporting sentence, and one primary call to action.
- Present one equal-weight three-node signal circuit. Compact nodes show icon,
  value, label, and reviewed short limit; native disclosures retain the full
  period, scope, attribution, qualifier, and exact Sources link.
- Present four lines of work as icon-led topic rails. Each line links to its
  filtered Work view and offers up to three approved topic filters with counts.
  Do not select, feature, or name a representative Home project.
- Treat compact signal limits and line/topic/icon mappings as protected
  presentation data, not editable copy.
- Remove duplicate trajectory, current-focus, archive-accounting, and methodology modules rather than rewriting all of them.

Preferred thesis pattern:

> I build the systems that make AI agents useful after the demo.

Treat that wording as fact-check copy, not an immutable slogan.

## Work index

- Let the color calendar and search carry the structure.
- Keep the Work overview free of first-person framing.
- Keep line, year, and topic filters visible; put secondary filters behind one disclosure.
- Send Home rails, calendar cells, search, organizations, and filters to the
  same Project index. The unfiltered overview does not show that index.
- Use a compact active-scope strip with independently removable line, topic,
  year, and organization filters.
- Render one selected line as a flat list. For cross-line searches, use simple
  non-collapsible line headings rather than nested disclosure wrappers.
- Write result rows as period + short title + either role or medium.
- If a child title matched, use one compact “Matched: …” note and still link to the canonical parent.
- Do not repeat form counts, category explanations, or archive totals in every row.

## Work detail

Keep the page a lean canonical evidence leaf, in this order:

1. Title and one what/why sentence.
2. Role, period, organization, and one primary artifact.
3. Line plus no more than three high-level topic links back to filtered Work.
4. Direct results, when present.
5. Collapsed additional artifacts, sources, and related context.

Do not repeat the title or summary, render empty ornamental sections, or turn
detail pages into category navigation. Give a single-artifact project one
primary artifact link rather than a duplicate artifact section.

## Sources

Prefer the visible title **What the numbers mean** and three sections:

1. Claims.
2. How I count.
3. Sources.

Keep counting rules neutral and short:

- Count each project once.
- Separate contribution from causality.
- Keep the limit beside the claim.

Do not restate the same limitation in accounting, non-claim, rule, caveat, and claim modules.

## Play

Use one literal object name and one personal description per doorway. The
literal name makes the room navigable; it is not the title of the experience
behind that object:

- **TV · Watch:** Anime I return to, what I’m watching, and the full shelf.
- **Bookshelf · Read:** Books in progress, finished, rated, and waiting.
- **Music player · Listen:** The tracks Spotify puts closest to the surface for me.
- **Record crate · Collect:** Records on my shelf, sorted by format and decade.
- **Computer · Games:** Games I own, games I return to, and rounded playtime.
- **Treadmill · Move:** Recent rides and runs, when Strava’s public feed is available.
- **Globe · Travel:** Places I’ve been, the photos I kept, and where they belong.

Give each object route its own authored screen title while keeping the literal
object visible in the doorway and room navigation:

- **TV:** Midnight Broadcast.
- **Bookshelf:** Bedside Shelf.
- **Music player:** Pocket Player.
- **Record crate:** Crate Archive.
- **Computer:** Desktop Library.
- **Treadmill:** Cardio Television.
- **Globe:** Postcard Planet.

Do not make an authored screen title replace the literal object name. Do not
make the screen eyebrow carry collection freshness, consent, or policy wording;
those exact semantics remain beside the relevant data or action.
Keep provider names out of editable screen-eyebrow fields. The closed room
registry appends the exact provider label at render time so attribution cannot
drift through a copy edit.

Prefer reader language:

- Overview / Browse all.
- Loading the collection…
- Updated [date].
- Open on [provider].

Keep consent, provider attribution, coverage, freshness, and recovery wording next to the data or action it governs. Do not move those semantics into decorative doorway copy.

## Simulation

- For v2.2 public and preview surfaces, Simulation is disabled: no invitation,
  route, API, sitemap entry, or client bundle is visible. Existing deck fields
  remain dormant preparation for a separately reviewed future activation.
- Keep `4N1SH` clearly framed as a generated game character, never Anish or an
  authoritative personal representative.
- Use editable copy for the invitation, instructions, nonfactual encounter
  flavor, and debrief framing.
- Keep claims in server-owned fact cards. Never place metrics, provider-derived
  facts, system instructions, safety behavior, model policy, or session/privacy
  rules into an editorial field.
- Preserve equivalent Action and Tactical instructions and an understandable
  authored-fallback path.

## Travel

- Use `Globe` as the literal room object and `Postcard Planet` as the authored
  screen title.
- Git-owned copy may frame the atlas and explain its interaction. Trip titles,
  dates, place labels, captions, tags, precision, and photo safety remain
  owner/fact-controlled in Travel Desk.
- Describe geography as approximate when the public contract does. Never imply
  live location, exact GPS, or comprehensive travel history.

## Metadata, errors, and accessibility

- Keep SEO descriptions evidence-backed and between the registry's minimum and maximum limits.
- Synchronize page metadata, JSON-LD, visible headings, and social descriptions.
- Keep visible labels and accessible names consistent. Do not use copy fields to change a purely programmatic accessible name.
- Make empty, stale, loading, unavailable, and 404 states say what happened and what the visitor can do next.
- Review line wrapping at 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. Shorten text before adding layout exceptions.
