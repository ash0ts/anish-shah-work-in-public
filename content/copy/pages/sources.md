## owner-reviewed-title
Owner-reviewed contributions

## owner-reviewed-body
Owner-reviewed entries describe curricula or internal tooling whose materials are not public; they are not independently inspectable source code. Status distinguishes developed material, merged implementation, and delivered work. Private records and repository links stay private.

## rule-1-title
Count each project once

## rule-1-body
Recordings, translations, mirrors, profiles, excerpts, and source pages do not inflate the total.

## rule-3-title
Separate contribution from causality

## rule-3-body
Individual contribution, program reach, supported pipeline, and organization outcomes remain separate.

## rule-4-title
Keep the limit beside the claim

## rule-4-body
Each derived measure keeps its period, source, attribution, rounding, and caveat.
