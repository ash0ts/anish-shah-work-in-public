## speaker-bio
Anish Shah builds agent infrastructure and evaluation workflows at Weights & Biases, part of CoreWeave. His engineering contributions span the W&B MCP Server's deployment and reliability, ARIA's streaming and recovery, and controlled studies of agent behavior. His talks use that work to explain how to trace failures, test tool interfaces, and decide what the evidence supports.

His path spans programming education, computer-vision research, analytics, recommendation systems, and production ML. He develops hands-on curricula and co-taught Fully Connected's Your Agent Learned to Cheat lab, where participants traced reward shortcuts and repaired the tests that missed them. He brings concrete implementations, experimental limits, and the engineering decisions behind them into each session.
