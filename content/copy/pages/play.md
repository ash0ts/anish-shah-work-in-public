## rig-note-fgn-dark-odyssey
My FGN six-string with Fishman Fluence Modern pickups installed. I call it the Dark Odyssey; the exact model code is still to be checked. The pickups are part of this guitar.

## rig-note-fender-duo-sonic
My gold-colored Fender Duo-Sonic. I think the finish is Desert Sand; the finish and year are unconfirmed. The catalog link covers the model family.

## rig-note-boss-ns2
Listens to the clean guitar, then closes after the drives and compressor. Reverb stays outside the gate.

## rig-note-kaede
An alternate overdrive voice, with bass, treble and selectable headroom. A choice alongside Galea and Lightspeed.

## rig-note-knife-drop
Octave, fuzz and synth textures. The space preset leaves the gate out so slower envelopes can breathe.

## rig-note-greer-lightspeed
My low-gain voice for edge and touch-sensitive rhythm sounds.

## rig-note-friedman-beod
The high-gain voice. I prefer the compressor after it to even the level and extend sustain.

## rig-note-keeley-compressor-plus
After the drives, where I like it. Blends compression with the dry signal to control level and sustain.

## rig-note-keeley-dyno-my-roto
Chorus, rotary and flange movement, after the gate.

## rig-note-swan-hunter
Lo-fi echo and reverb for the moments that need space. A situational texture rather than an always-on delay.

## rig-note-harmonic-tremolo-unconfirmed
Rhythmic movement after the gate. A separate texture to call up when the part needs a pulse.

## rig-note-tuner-unconfirmed
My tuner, connected to the G3S AUX output rather than inserted among the effect loops.

## rig-note-boss-rc1
Owned and kept off this board plan. Still available for straightforward looping in another setup.

## rig-note-op1-field
External recorder and instrument. Receives OPUS headphone audio; headphones move to the OP-1 while recording.

## rig-note-orange-crush-20rt
My real amp, fed at the front input from the isolated G3S output. This model has no effects loop.

## rig-note-empress-paraeq
Planned final tone shaping in loop 12, after modulation and space.

## rig-note-vemuram-galea
Planned overdrive voice for the Galea presets. Purchase completion is still to be confirmed.

## rig-note-gigrig-genx14
Planned isolated power distribution. Its supplied 24 V adapter feeds the system; pedals use rated DC outputs.

## rig-note-gigrig-genx4lc
Four-output power expansion for the GENX14 system. It does not add audio loops to the G3S.

## rig-note-gigrig-g3s
Recalls selected loops and their order. Presets do not move the pedals’ physical knobs.

## rig-note-schmidt-sa550xdm
Ordered from Schmidt Array. The two-floor layout is planned; final plug clearance is checked by the builder.

## rig-note-lehle-dual-expression
Planned expression control through G3S. It can send an assigned MIDI value without carrying guitar audio.

## rig-note-cme-h4midi-wc
Routes G3S commands to OPUS and the external MIDI port. The proposed power combiner still needs builder approval.

## rig-note-cme-c2midi-pro
Planned DIN-to-USB MIDI interface. OP-1 field hosts it for standalone foot control.

## rig-note-canvas-power-6
My existing Walrus Canvas supply, kept off the main board. I have called it Power 6; the exact model needs checking before adding a catalog link.

## rig-note-genx14-adapter
Included with GENX14. The lower deck reserves space for the adapter; this is not a separate pedal purchase.

## rig-note-schmidt-connector-box
Board-edge access for guitar, amp and the OPUS headphone feed. Installation details remain part of the build order.

## rig-note-schmidt-rear-patchbay
Rear connections for OPUS line out and auxiliary input, with two spare channels.

## rig-note-schmidt-socket-plate
Planned XLR, MIDI and two separate USB data connections. Final hardware configuration needs supplier confirmation.

## rig-note-photo-accessories-unconfirmed
